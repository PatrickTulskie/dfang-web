About

dfang defangs indicators of compromise — URLs, email addresses, IP addresses — so you can share them without anyone accidentally clicking a live link or a chat app helpfully unfurling a preview of a malware site. rfang puts the bite back when you need the original.

Everything on this site runs locally in your browser: the same Rust code behind the dfang and rfang CLIs, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

API

There's also a small API: POST plain text and get it back transformed. Unlike the page above, this does send your text through Cloudflare, so keep genuinely sensitive IOC lists in the browser boxes. Nothing is stored.

curl --data-binary 'http://evil.example.com' https://dfang.sh/api/defang
hxxp[://]evil[.]example[.]com

curl --data-binary 'hxxp[://]evil[.]example[.]com' https://dfang.sh/api/refang
http://evil.example.com

Bodies can be multi-line (--data-binary @iocs.txt works), responses are text/plain, CORS is open, and bodies are capped at 1,000,000 characters.
