Privacy

dfang doesn't collect, store, or transmit any data. Everything you paste into the app or its share extension is processed on your device and never leaves it. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs.

The dfang.sh website works the same way: it runs locally in your browser. The optional API at dfang.sh/api is separate and sends the text you submit through Cloudflare to process it.
